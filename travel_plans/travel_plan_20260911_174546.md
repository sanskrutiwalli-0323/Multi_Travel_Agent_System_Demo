## DAY‑BY‑DAY ITINERARY  

### **Day 1 – Arrival & North‑Goa Vibes**  
**Morning**  
- **Arrive at Dabolim Airport** (or fly into Goa via a connecting flight).  
- Pick up your rental car/scooter (book in advance – Maruti Swift, Hyundai i20, or a 150 cc scooter).  
- Drive to **Calangute** (≈1 h).  

**Check‑in suggestions** (choose one based on budget):  
| Category | Hotel | Approx. Rate (USD/night) | Why |
|----------|-------|--------------------------|-----|
| **Mid‑range** | **Resort Rio** (Calangute) | $70‑90 | Central location, pool, easy beach access. |
| **Boutique** | **Casa De Goa** (Panaji) – if you prefer a heritage stay for the night. | $120‑150 | Portuguese‑style villa, great for a romantic vibe. |
| **Budget** | **Zostel Goa** (Baga) | $25‑35 | Dorm & private rooms, social vibe for solo travelers. |

**Afternoon**  
- **Lunch** at *Mum’s Kitchen* (Calangute) – authentic Goan thali.  
- **Beach time**: Sunbathe at Calangute → walk to Baga (≈15 min). Try water‑sports (parasailing, jet‑ski) if you’re adventurous.  

**Evening**  
- **Sunset at Chapora Fort** (≈30 min drive from Baga). The fort offers sweeping views over Vagator Beach – perfect for photos.  
- **Dinner** at **Thalassa** (Vagator) – Greek‑Mediterranean with live music and a spectacular sea view.  

**Night** – Rest early; you’ll have a few hours of driving tomorrow.

---

### **Day 2 – Cultural Immersion in Panaji & Old Goa**  
**Morning**  
- **Breakfast** at the hotel (most include a continental spread).  
- **Drive to Fort Aguada** (≈30 min). Explore the 17th‑century lighthouse and the fort’s sea‑level ramp.  

**Mid‑morning**  
- **Candolim Beach** – a quick dip or a relaxed stroll.  

**Lunch**  
- **Vinayak Family Restaurant** (Candolim) – famous for Goan fish curry and prawn balchão.  

**Afternoon – Old Goa**  
- **Drive to Old Goa** (≈45 min).  
- **Visit the UNESCO‑listed churches:**  
  - **Basilica of Bom Jesus** (holds St. Francis Xavier’s relic).  
  - **Se Cathedral** (one of Asia’s largest churches).  
  - **Church of St. Catherine** (great for a quiet moment).  

**Evening**  
- **River cruise on the Mandovi** – book a **Mandovi Dinner Cruise** (≈2 h). Enjoy Goan folk dance, live band, and a seafood buffet as the sun sets over the river.  

**Night** – Stay in **Panaji** (e.g., **Casa De Goa** or **The Crown Goa**, a boutique hotel near the river).  

---

### **Day 3 – Spice, Waterfalls & Jungle**  
**Early Morning**  
- **Breakfast** at the hotel.  
- **Drive to a Spice Plantation** (e.g., **Sahakari Spice Farm**, 30 min from Panaji).  
- **Guided tour (≈2 h)** – learn about pepper, cardamom, nutmeg, and sample fresh coconut water.  

**Mid‑Morning**  
- Continue to **Dudhsagar Falls** (≈2 h from the plantation).  
- **Option A – Jeep Safari** (recommended): Book a 4‑WD jeep from **Dudhsagar Jeep Safari** (₹2,200 ≈ $28 per vehicle).  
- **Option B – Trek** (moderate difficulty, 2 h each way).  

**Afternoon**  
- **Picnic lunch** at the base of the falls (packed from the plantation or local dhaba).  
- **Explore the surrounding Bhagwan Mahavir Wildlife Sanctuary** – keep an eye out for bison, deer, and exotic birds.  

**Evening**  
- Return to **Panaji** (≈2 h 30 m).  
- **Dinner** at **Mum’s Kitchen** (Panaji) for a second taste of authentic Goan cuisine.  

**Night** – Stay the same as Day 2.

---

### **Day 4 – South‑Goa Beach Bliss**  
**Morning**  
- **Check‑out** early and head south (≈3 h 30 m).  
- **Stop at Cabo de Rama Fort** (near Canacona) – a dramatic cliff‑side fort with panoramic ocean views.  

**Mid‑Morning**  
- Continue to **Palolem Beach** (≈30 min).  

**Check‑in suggestions** (South‑Goa):  
| Category | Hotel | Approx. Rate (USD/night) | Why |
|----------|-------|--------------------------|-----|
| **Luxury** | **Alila Diwa Goa** (Majorda) | $250‑300 | Infinity pool, beachfront, spa. |
| **Mid‑range** | **Art Resort Goa** (Palolem) | $80‑110 | Boutique, beachfront cottages, yoga. |
| **Budget** | **Zostel Palolem** | $30‑45 | Dorm & private rooms, social vibe. |

**Lunch**  
- **Café del Mar** (Palolem) – fresh seafood, salads, and coconut water.  

**Afternoon**  
- **Beach time** – rent a **kayak** or **stand‑up paddle board** to explore the calm waters.  
- **Optional**: Take a short boat ride to **Butterfly Beach** (accessible only by sea; about 30 min).  

**Evening**  
- **Sunset at Cola Beach** (≈15 min drive) – a secluded crescent with a lagoon, perfect for a quiet moment.  
- **Dinner** at **Martin’s Corner** (Betalbatim, 20 min north) – legendary Goan prawns and chicken xacuti.  

**Night** – Return to Palolem for a relaxed night.  

---

### **Day 5 – Chill & Departure**  
**Morning**  
- **Beach‑side brunch** at **Art Resort’s restaurant** (if staying there) or **Café 105** (Palolem). Try the **Goan poee** (bread) with **chorizo** and fresh fruit juices.  

**Late Morning**  
- **Leisurely stroll** through **Colva Market** (≈30 min drive) – pick up spices, cashews, and local handicrafts.  

**Early Afternoon**  
- **Check‑out** and drive back to Dabolim Airport (≈1 h 30 m). Return the rental car.  

**Optional** (if your flight is later):  
- Quick dip at **Cavelossim Beach** or a **spa treatment** at the airport lounge.  

**Fly home** with a sun‑kissed glow and a belly full of Goan flavours!

### 4. **Driving Tips & Road Safety**  

1. **Drive on the left.** Keep an eye on motorbikes—Goa’s roads are peppered with two‑wheelers.  
2. **Speed limits:** 50 km/h inside towns, 80 km/h on highways.  
3. **Parking:** Use designated parking lots near beaches; avoid parking on the road during peak hours.  
4. **Fuel stations:** Available every 30‑40 km; keep the tank at least half full when heading to remote spots (Dudhsagar).  
5. **Night driving:** Not recommended on narrow coastal roads after 9 pm due to poor lighting and stray animals.

### 6. **Safety & Health**
