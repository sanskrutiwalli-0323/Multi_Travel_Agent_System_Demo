## KEY TIPS TO STAY **UNDER ₹2 Lakh**

1. **Book flights early (2–3 months)** – use price‑alert tools (Google Flights, Skyscanner).  
2. **Purchase the JR Pass before you leave India** (online) – cheaper than buying individual Shinkansen tickets.  
3. **Stay in business‑hotel chains** (Toyoko Inn, APA, Hotel Mystays) – clean, central, breakfast included.  
4. **Eat like a local:** convenience‑store bento, street‑food stalls, and set‑menus (≈ ¥1 200).  
5. **Use prepaid IC cards** (Suica/Pasmo) for sub‑way & bus rides; they’re refundable for a small fee.  
6. **Combine attractions:** many temples have free entry; buy combination tickets where available (e.g., Osaka Museum of History + Osaka Castle).  
7. **Travel light** – avoid extra baggage fees; most Japanese hotels store luggage for free.
