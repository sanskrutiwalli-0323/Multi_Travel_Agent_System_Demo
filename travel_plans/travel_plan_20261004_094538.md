## 1️⃣ DAY‑BY‑DAY DETAILED ITINERARY  

### Day 1 – Arrival & First Impressions  
**Morning**  
- **Arrive in Paris** (Charles‑de‑Gaulle or Orly).  
- Take the **RER B** train to central Paris (≈30 min).  
- Check‑in to **Hôtel Malte – Astotel** (7‑Ave. de la République, 2nd arrondissement).  

**Afternoon**  
- Grab a quick lunch at **Le Petit Fer à Cheval** (classic bistro, near Hôtel Malte).  
- Stroll **Île de la Cité**:  
  - **Notre‑Dame** (exterior, as interior restoration continues).  
  - **Sainte‑Chapelle** (stunning stained‑glass).  
- Wander the **Latin Quarter** – explore Rue Mouffetard’s market stalls.  

**Evening**  
- Dinner at **Chez Janou** (Provençal cuisine, lively atmosphere).  
- Optional: early night or a relaxed walk along the **Seine** to see the city lights.

---

### Day 2 – The Iconic Paris  
**Morning – Eiffel Tower**  
- Pre‑book a **skip‑the‑line ticket** for the Eiffel Tower (choose “ summit” for the full view).  
- Arrive ~08:30 am to avoid crowds.  

**Mid‑day – Musée d’Orsay**  
- Take the **RER C** to Musée d’Orsay (10‑min walk).  
- Highlights: Monet’s “Water Lilies,” Van Gogh’s “Starry Night Over the Rhône.”  

**Lunch**  
- **Café Campana** inside the museum (great view of the Seine).  

**Afternoon – Champ de Mars Picnic**  
- Pick up baguettes, cheese, and fruit from a nearby **boulangerie** (e.g., **Le Grenier à Pain**).  
- Picnic on the **Champ de Mars** with the tower as backdrop.  

**Evening – Dinner & Nightlife**  
- Dine at **Le Jules Verne** (restaurant on the Eiffel Tower) *or* **Le Comptoir du Relais** for a more affordable bistro experience.  
- If you have energy, head to **Le Marais** for a cocktail at **Candelaria** (hidden speakeasy).

---

### Day 3 – Art, History & Shopping  
**Morning – Louvre Museum**  
- Arrive right at opening (09:00 am).  
- Buy a **ticket with timed entry** online.  
- Must‑see: Mona Lisa, Winged Victory, Egyptian Antiquities.  

**Late Morning – Palais Royal & Gardens**  
- Walk through the **Palais Royal** courtyard (the iconic black-and-white columns).  

**Lunch**  
- **Café Marly** (views of the Louvre’s pyramid) – upscale but worth the treat.  

**Afternoon – Le Marais**  
- Explore the historic **Le Marais** district:  
  - **Place des Vosges** (oldest square).  
  - Boutique shopping (Merci, The Broken Arm).  
  - **Musée Picasso** (if you love modern art).  

**Evening – Dinner in Le Marais**  
- **L’As du Fallafel** (famous falafel sandwich) for a casual bite.  
- For a sit‑down meal, try **Les Philosophes** (classic French bistro).  

**Night**  
- Return to **Hôtel Astra Opéra – Astotel** (if you prefer to switch hotels for a change of scenery) – it’s a 5‑star, 5.0‑rating property near Opéra Garnier, perfect for a night out in the 9th arrondissement.

---

### Day 4 – Bohemian Montmartre & Riverside Chill  
**Morning – Montmartre**  
- Take Metro Line 2 to **Anvers** or **Abbesses**.  
- Climb (or take the funicular) to **Sacré‑Cœur Basilica** – panoramic city view.  
- Wander the **Place du Tertre** – artists painting portraits.  

**Brunch**  
- **Le Consulat** (historic café) or **Hardware Société** for a modern brunch.  

**Afternoon – Canal Saint‑Martin**  
- Metro to **Jaurès** (Line 5).  
- Walk along the **Canal Saint‑Martin**: trendy boutiques, street art, and cafés.  
- Stop at **Le Verre Volé** for a glass of natural wine and cheese.  

**Evening – Seine River Cruise**  
- Board a **Bateaux‑Mouches** or **Vedettes de Paris** cruise (departure near **Pont de l’Alma**).  
- 1‑hour dinner cruise (optional) – see illuminated monuments from the water.  

**Late Night**  
- Return to **Hyatt Paris Madeleine** (central, elegant, close to many metro lines).  
- Nightcap at **Bar Hemingway** in the Ritz (if you’re feeling extra luxurious).

---

### Day 5 – Last‑Minute Pleasures & Departure  
**Morning**  
- Breakfast at a local boulangerie (e.g., **Du Pain et des Idées** – try the “pain au chocolat”).  
- Visit **Marché d’Aligre** (open daily) for fresh produce, cheese, and souvenirs.  

**Mid‑day**  
- If time permits, pop into **Musée de l’Orangerie** (Monet’s Water Lilies) or **Musée Rodin** (The Thinker).  

**Lunch**  
- Quick bite at **L’Entrecôte** (famous for steak‑frites).  

**Afternoon**  
- Return to hotel, collect luggage.  
- Take the **RER B** or a pre‑booked airport shuttle to the airport.  

**Departure**  
- Arrive at the airport at least **2 hours** before your flight.
